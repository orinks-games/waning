# Waning

## How a run goes

You start in heaven and descend with all of its strength. The world below
has eight areas, each 80 metres long with a gate at the right end. Every
gate you pass takes a quarter of your power and your health, so you hit
hardest at the top and grow weaker the deeper you go.

Enemies drop souls when they die. When you fall, your souls rise with you
and become grace, which buys blessings in heaven for the next descent.

## Controls

- Left and Right arrows walk.
- Space attacks the way you are facing.
- Up arrow jumps. Jump over an enemy to get past it, or jump when it cries
  out to dodge its blow.
- S reads your health, power, souls and the distance to the gate.
- D names the nearest enemy, how far away it is, and its health.
- Escape pauses.

Up and Down move through a menu, Enter chooses, and Escape goes back. F1
reads help for wherever you are, and F2 stops speech. Settings, then
Keyboard shortcuts, lets you move any game control to another key.

## Listening

Each enemy keeps making its sound, so you can hear where it is: slimes
bubble, shades whisper, and ogres stomp. When one cries out, it strikes
about a second later. Ogres wind up the longest and shades the shortest.

Your blows sound lower and quieter as you weaken. The gate rings when you
are within 40 metres of it.

## Enemies

- Slime: weak and slow.
- Shade: fast, and gives more souls.
- Ogre: slow and tough. It hits hard, barely moves when struck, and gives
  the most souls.

Deeper areas have more enemies, and they hit harder and take more blows.

## Blessings

- Might adds 15 to your starting power per level.
- Vigor adds 25 to your starting health per level.
- Steadfast makes each gate take 3 percent less of your strength per level.
- Mercy heals 15 percent of your health at each gate per level.

Each level costs more grace than the last. Grace and blessings are saved
when you buy and when a run ends.

## Speech

If you cannot hear the game, press Control, Shift, and V together to select
the built-in voice. Press it again to return to automatic speech selection.
