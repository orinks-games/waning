## 0.1.0

The first release.

- Descend through twelve stages in four regions, with a boss at the bottom of each, losing 15 percent of your power and health at every gate.
- Walk, run, jump, dash, block, parry and charge heavy blows.
- Fight slimes, shades, ogres, wraiths and mimics, with tougher elites among them.
- Cross pits and dodge spike traps.
- Collect gold, potions, and weapons, armor and charms in three rarities.
- Shop with the merchant in each region, and use wells, altars and chests.
- Find ten relics, buy four blessings in heaven, and hear the Keeper's story between runs.
- Continue a descent from the start of the stage you left.
- Lengthen warnings, slow the game, or turn on always running in Settings.
