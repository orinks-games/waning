# Waning

## About the game

Heaven's Lamp is going dark, and at the bottom of the world the Hollow King
is drinking its light. You go down with all the strength the Lamp has left,
through twelve stages and four bosses. Every gate takes some of your
strength. When you fall, the souls you carried become grace, which buys
blessings in heaven for the next descent. Beat the Hollow King to win.

## How a descent goes

A descent has four regions: the Bright Fields, the Hollow Wood, the Drowned
Steps and the Cinder Stair. Each has two areas, 80 metres long, and a boss
arena of 30 metres. The gate is at the right end of each, and rings as you
get close. A boss holds its gate shut until it falls.

Every gate takes 15 percent of your power and health, so you hit hardest at
the top. Areas are laid out fresh each descent.

Your descent is saved each time a stage begins. Continue descent, on the
title menu, takes you back to the start of that stage. Descending again from
heaven starts a new run in its place.

## Moving

Hold Left or Right to walk, and hold Shift as well to run. Running steps
come quicker. The Always run setting swaps the two.

A jump carries you over an enemy, a pit or a blow. A running jump goes
further.

A dash is a quick rush of about 3 metres the way you hold, or the way you
face. Nothing can hurt you while you dash, and you pass straight through
enemies. After a dash you need a moment before the next.

## Pits and spikes

A pit is a gap in the ground with wind blowing up out of it. Walk into one
and you lose some health and climb back out on the side you came from. Jump
or dash across.

A spike trap clicks, and a moment later the spikes rise with a scrape of
steel. Be out of reach, or in the air, when they rise. A block does not stop
them.

## Fighting

Attack strikes the way you face. Hold Attack after a blow and a metal ring
tells you a heavy blow is ready; let go to swing it. A heavy blow does two
and a half times the damage, hits everything in reach, throws enemies back
and stops an enemy that is about to strike.

Hold Block to raise your guard. You cannot move while it is up, but you can
turn. A block takes most of a blow from the side you face. Raise it just
before the blow lands and you parry: you take nothing, and the enemy reels.
A parried boss loses its next move.

When you are struck, the game says your health.

## Enemies

Every enemy keeps making its sound, so you can hear where it is. When one
cries out, it strikes about a second later: jump, dash, block, or step back
out of reach.

- Slime: bubbles. Weak and slow.
- Shade: moans. Fast.
- Ogre: stomps and breathes. Slow and tough, hits hard and barely moves when
  struck.
- Wraith: a hollow wind. Keeps its distance and throws a burning bolt after
  its cry. Jump as the bolt reaches you, block it, or close in.

An elite sounds lower and deeper than the rest of its kind. It takes more
blows, hits harder, is worth three times the souls, and always leaves gear.

## Bosses

Each boss has its own sound, and warns before every attack:

- Its roar: a slam. Jump, or be more than three metres away.
- A deep bellow: a charge across the arena. Jump or dash as the rumble
  reaches you.
- A rush of fire: a bolt. Jump as it arrives.
- A howl: it calls up two spawn.
- A swish: it vanishes, then comes back a few metres from you with a chime.

At half health a boss grows faster, and its bolts come two at a time. A boss
leaves rare gear or better, a pile of gold, and a choice of three relics.

## Gold, potions and gear

Every enemy drops gold, with a clink of coins. Gold buys from the merchant.
Whatever gold you still carry when the run ends goes home with you, 5 gold
to a soul.

You start each run with 1 potion and can carry 3. Drink one to heal 35
percent of your health. Enemies now and then leave a potion, picked up at
once.

Gear lies on the ground with a soft glittering sound. Walk up to it and press
Use. Gear for an empty slot goes on at once. Otherwise you hear what it does
and choose to take it, leaving yours on the ground, or leave it.

There are three slots. A weapon adds damage to every blow, and no gate takes
it. Armor takes a share off every blow. A charm does one of three things: a
hawk charm makes some blows strike twice as hard, a leech charm heals you a
little for each blow you land, and a lucky coin brings more gold. Gear is
common, rare or legendary, and gets stronger the deeper you find it.

The inventory lists your gear, potions, gold, souls and relics.

## Wells, altars, chests and the merchant

Each area has a well, an altar or a chest, somewhere past the start. Walk up
to its sound and press Use.

- A well, running water, heals a third of your health.
- An altar, a low hum, takes a quarter of your health and offers a choice of
  three relics.
- A chest, rattling chains, holds souls, gold and sometimes gear. Some
  chests bite.

The second area of each region has a merchant, the sound of a shaken purse.
The merchant sells potions, mending to full health, one piece of rare gear
or better, and one relic. Gear you buy goes on at once.

## Relics

A relic lasts until the run ends.

- Ember Edge: your blows deal a quarter more damage.
- Thorn Mail: anything that strikes you takes a third of your power in
  return.
- Feather Boots: jumps last longer.
- Hungry Blade: each kill heals 4 health.
- Miser's Purse: enemies drop half again as many souls.
- Quick Step: you walk a third faster.
- Echo Charm: every third swing strikes twice.
- Ward Stone: the first blow in each area is turned aside.
- Last Ember: once, a killing blow leaves you at 1 health instead.
- Anchor: the next gate takes none of your strength.

## Blessings

- Might adds 15 to your starting power per level.
- Vigor adds 25 to your starting health per level.
- Steadfast lowers what each gate takes by 2 and a half percent per level.
- Mercy heals 15 percent of your health at each gate per level.

Each level costs more grace than the last. The Keeper, in the heaven menu,
repeats the last thing the Keeper told you.

## Settings

Besides speech, volume and the controls, Settings has three rows for this
game.

- Warning time: longer gives you 40 percent more time between a warning and
  the blow.
- Game speed: 100, 85 or 70 percent. Slows the whole world, you included.
- Always run: run without holding Shift. Holding Shift then walks.

## Speech

If you cannot hear the game, press Control, Shift, and V together to select
the built-in voice. Press it again to return to automatic speech selection.

## Controls

These are the default keys. If you change them in Settings, the game's
spoken instructions follow your bindings.

- Left and Right arrows: walk. Hold Shift with them to run.
- Space: attack. Hold it after a blow for a heavy blow.
- Up arrow: jump.
- Z: dash.
- X: hold to block.
- Q: drink a potion.
- Down arrow: use a well, altar, chest or merchant, or pick up gear.
- I: inventory.
- S: health, power, potions, gold, souls, the stage, the distance to the
  gate, and anything left to use or pick up.
- D: the nearest enemy, its distance and its health.
- Escape: pause.

On a controller: the D-pad walks, X attacks, A jumps, B dashes, the left
bumper blocks, D-pad up drinks, D-pad down uses, Y opens the inventory, and
the left and right stick buttons read your status and the nearest enemy.

In menus, Up and Down move, Enter chooses, and Escape goes back. F1 reads
help for wherever you are, and F2 stops speech.
